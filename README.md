## About Me

I am a graduate student at the School of Software, Northwestern Polytechnical University.

My research interests focus on computer vision and long-range UAV vision-language navigation.

## Research Interests

- Computer Vision
- Object Detection
- Vision-Language Navigation
- Long-Range UAV Navigation
- Spatiotemporal Graph Representation
- Long-Term Memory and Temporal Reasoning

## Research Overview

My research focuses on long-range UAV vision-language navigation, particularly cross-region, multi-landmark, and multi-stage tasks.

I am interested in exploring how long-term memory and spatiotemporal graphs can support navigation tasks by modeling spatial relationships, accessibility, temporal continuity, and task states. My goal is to improve route planning, progress estimation, and navigation recovery in complex environments.
